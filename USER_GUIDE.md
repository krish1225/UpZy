# FwF User Guide

## Getting Started with FwF

Welcome to **FwF** – Your step tracking and fitness competition app! This guide will help you get started.

---

## 1. How to Login

### First Time Users
1. Open the app at [https://krish1225.github.io/UpZy/](https://krish1225.github.io/UpZy/)
2. Click **"Join Challenge"** button
3. Click **"Sign Up"** link at the bottom
4. Enter:
   - Your email address
   - Your invite code (ask your challenge organizer)
   - Create a password (at least 6 characters)
   - Confirm your password
5. Click **"Create Account"**

### Existing Users
1. Click **"Join Challenge"** button
2. Enter your email and password
3. Click **"Continue"**
4. You'll see your dashboard

### Forgot Password?
1. Click **"Forgot Password?"** on the login page
2. Enter your email
3. Create a new password
4. Click **"Reset Password"**

---

## 2. How to Log Steps

### Daily Submission
1. After logging in, look for the **"Submit Today"** section on the left
2. Enter your **steps** for the day
3. Enter your current **weight (lbs)**
4. The **date** auto-fills with today's date (change if needed)
5. Click **"Submit"**

### That's it! 
Your data is saved and appears in your history table below.

---

## 3. How to Use the Leaderboard

### View Rankings
1. Click the **"Leaderboard"** tab at the bottom
2. Select a challenge from the **dropdown menu** (only active challenges show)
3. View three types of rankings:
   - **Daily Winner** - Today's top performer
   - **Weekly Ranking** - This week's leaderboard
   - **Overall** - Full challenge ranking

### Reading the Chart
- **Taller bars = more steps**
- Your position shows your rank in the challenge
- Updates daily as users submit their steps

---

## 4. Dashboard Overview

### Left Side - Submit Activity
- Quick form to log today's steps and weight
- Keep it updated daily for the best experience

### Middle - Your Stats
- **Today** - Steps walked today
- **This Year** - Total steps in 2026

### Right Side - Weight Trend
- **Last Month** (default) - See your recent weight changes
- **Last Year** - Toggle to view full year trend
- Green line shows your weight pattern

### Below - Daily History
- All your previous submissions
- Date, steps, and weight recorded

---

## 5. Quick Tips

✅ **Submit daily** - Don't miss a day to maintain your streak!  
✅ **Check the leaderboard** - See how you stack against friends  
✅ **Use the month view** - Easy way to track weight changes  
✅ **Log in regularly** - Your data updates automatically
